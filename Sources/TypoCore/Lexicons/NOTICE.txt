English and Russian word-frequency lists adapted from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave.
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/tree/525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6/content/2018
Source corpus: OpenSubtitles 2018 (https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles-v2018.php).
License for the original and adapted lists: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Adaptation: normalized case, restricted each list to its native alphabet and 3–40 letters,
kept words attested at least 25 times, deduplicated, and sorted alphabetically.
